# IRS 2026 Forms, Instructions, and Publications Discovery

Research date: 2026-11-12 (America/Los_Angeles).
Method: computer-agent browsing in native Safari, starting at the IRS forms and
instructions hub. Compared the current-products listing, draft listing, and
post-release corrections against `corpora/irs-2026/source-catalog.json`.
Scope: federal self-employment and ordinary-income planning for income earned
during calendar year 2026, including estimated payments made during that year.

This is a discovery record, not an ingestion manifest or tax-law approval. Listing
metadata was inspected; newly discovered PDFs were not downloaded, hashed, or
compared page by page in this pass. The search was targeted, not an exhaustive
review of all 467 products returned by the current listing's “2026” search.

- [Changes to current forms and publications](https://www.irs.gov/forms-pubs/changes-to-current-forms-publications)
  (page reviewed/updated November 10, 2026).

Revision and posted dates below are separate fields from the IRS tables. A
document posted in 2026 can still concern tax year 2025. A current-listing entry
also needs inspection of its printed revision and effective dates before it
becomes calculation authority. Drafts remain review inputs; IRS warns against
relying on draft instructions/publications or filing draft forms.

The planning year must remain the calendar income year: a user estimating 2026
self-employment taxes during 2026 needs 2026 rules, even while the hub prominently
offers 2025 return materials. Form publication dates must not choose the session's
tax year.

## Corrections that need independent provenance

### 2026 Form 1040-ES SALT correction: opened and read

[IRS correction notice](https://www.irs.gov/forms-pubs/correction-to-state-and-local-income-tax-deduction-amount-in-the-2026-form-1040-es)
(index date February 25, 2026; page reviewed March 16, 2026) corrects the reminder
to a 2026 SALT limit of $40,400 ($20,200 married filing separately), reduced when
MAGI exceeds $505,000 ($252,500 married filing separately), with floors of $10,000
($5,000 married filing separately).

IRS explicitly states: **“The 2026 Form 1040-ES will not be revised.”**

Consequently, an unchanged PDF hash does not establish that all source guidance
is unchanged. The existing monitor watches the corrections index; the individual
correction page also needs an independently pinned snapshot and a link to the
source it corrects. The current standard-deduction planning scope does not by
itself support an itemized SALT calculation; record this correction without
implying that itemization is implemented.

### Form 8995 taxable income before QBI: opened and read

[IRS correction notice](https://www.irs.gov/forms-pubs/corrections-to-the-instructions-on-how-to-calculate-the-taxable-income-before-qbi-deduction-for-form-8995)
(index date February 13, 2026; page reviewed June 28, 2026) corrects the first two
bullets of the page 4, line 11 instructions. For Form 1040/1040-SR, the corrected
calculation uses line 11a less lines 12e and 13b. For 1040-NR it uses line 11a less
lines 12, 13b, and 13c.

Tie this correction to the actual revision it corrects; its publication during
2026 does not establish that those are 2026 return line references. Review whether
the planner's simplified AGI-minus-standard-deduction base adequately describes
its supported deductions and exclusions. Do not copy these line numbers into the
2026 graph without the corresponding final form/instructions review.

1. **Complete the core evidence set:** acquire and hash 2026 Schedule C and SE
   forms; verify the current Schedule SE instructions against the pinned copy.
   Pin the 1040-ES SALT and address correction pages, preserving the relationship
   between unchanged PDFs and later corrections.

3. **Reconcile QBI and payment guidance:** inspect the 8995/8995-A corrections,
   review 2026 draft deltas without promoting them, and ingest Publication 509 for
   payment-calendar review. Keep income year, payment due date, and processing
   year separate in metadata.
4. **Review adjacent supported features:** evaluate W-2/withholding corrections,
   mileage, home-office, retirement, and special-deduction implications against
   explicit planner scope. Record unsupported cases as actionable limitations.
5. **Promote only after verification:** extract each acquired source, retain exact
   citations and correction precedence, review source-to-rule changes, run
   calculation regressions for affected facts, and rebuild the review release.
   Keep human approval separate from discovery and draft comparison.
